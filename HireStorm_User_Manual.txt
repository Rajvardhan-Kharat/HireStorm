SECTION 1: GETTING STARTED — REGISTRATION & LOGIN
================================================================

1.1 CREATING A STUDENT ACCOUNT
-------------------------------
1. Open HireStorm in your browser.
2. Click "Register" on the login page.
3. Fill in:
   - First Name, Last Name
   - Email address
   - Password (min 8 characters)
   - Select Role: STUDENT
4. Click "Create Account".
5. You are redirected to your Student Dashboard.

TIP: Use a professional email (college or Gmail). Avoid temporary emails.

1.2 CREATING A COMPANY ACCOUNT
-------------------------------
1. Click "Register" on the login page.
2. Fill in your details and select Role: COMPANY.
3. After registering, you must complete company profile setup.
4. Your account will be in PENDING state until a Platform Admin approves it.
5. Once APPROVED, you get full access to Company Dashboard.

NOTE: Company accounts require admin verification. This usually takes 24 hours.

1.3 LOGGING IN
--------------
1. Go to the Login page.
2. Enter your Email and Password.
3. Click "Login".
4. The system auto-redirects based on your role:
   - Students  → /dashboard
   - Companies → /company/dashboard
   - Admins    → /admin/dashboard

1.4 COLLEGE TPO LOGIN (Separate Portal)
----------------------------------------
Colleges use a SEPARATE login page.
1. Go to: /college/login
2. Enter your College Code and Password (provided by HireStorm admin).
3. You are redirected to your College Portal.

2.1 STUDENT DASHBOARD (/dashboard)

      - Shows company, Day X of 90, stipend.

--------------------------------------------
1. Click "Listings" in the sidebar or "Browse Jobs" on the dashboard.

2.3 VIEWING A LISTING DETAIL (/listings/:id)
---------------------------------------------
The detail page shows:

To Apply:
1. Click "Apply Now".

4. You receive a confirmation toast notification.

2.4 MY APPLICATIONS (/my-applications)

Columns / Statuses:

  - OFFER — you have been given an offer.

2.5 PROFILE (/profile)
------------------------
Your professional profile visible to companies.

Sections:
  [LEFT COLUMN]
  - Avatar (initials-based, with gradient)
  - Profile Strength meter (0–100%) — fill all fields to hit 100%.
  - Social Links: LinkedIn, GitHub, Portfolio.

  [RIGHT COLUMN]
  - Personal Info: First/Last Name, Phone, Bio.
  - Education: Institution name, Degree.
  - Skills: Dynamic skill tags.

How to Edit:
1. Click the pencil (✏️) icon on any section.
2. Make changes.
3. Click "Save Changes" (top right) to save all edits.

Adding Skills:
1. Click ✏️ on Skills section.
2. Type a skill name (e.g. "React") in the input box.
3. Press Enter or click the + button.
4. To remove a skill, click the × on the skill chip.
5. Save changes.

TIP: A complete profile (100%) makes you more visible to company recruiters.

SECTION 3: INTERNSHIP LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT (ILM)
================================================================
Only accessible after ACCEPTING an internship offer. Role changes to INTERN.

3.1 ILM DASHBOARD (/ilm)
--------------------------
Your 90-day internship command center.

Top Section:
- Internship progress bar (Day X of 90, % complete).
- Start date and end date.
- Status badge: ACTIVE / COMPLETED.

Stat Cards:
- Daily Logs Submitted (out of days elapsed).
- WBS Weeks Done (out of 13 weeks).
- CA Score (your Continuous Assessment %).
- Stipend (₹/month).

Mentor Card:
- Name and email of your assigned mentor.

Monthly Reviews:
- Month 1, 2, 3 review scores (out of 100).
- Pending or Completed status.

Exam & Certificate Section (unlocked after 90 days + CA threshold):
- "Final Exam Unlocked" — click "Start Exam" to attempt.
- Pass score: 70% | One attempt only.
- If passed → "Certificate Ready!" with Download PDF button.
- Share on LinkedIn button for direct LinkedIn posting.

3.2 DAILY LOG (/ilm/daily-log)
--------------------------------
Submit your daily work log. Must be submitted every working day.

Form fields:
- Tasks Completed (text area)
- Blockers / Issues (text area)
- Plan for Tomorrow (text area)

Submit by clicking "Submit Daily Log".
Once submitted, the button on Dashboard shows "✅ Log Submitted Today".

NOTE: Mentors review daily logs and give scores. Missed logs hurt your CA score.

3.3 WORK BREAKDOWN STRUCTURE (/ilm/wbs)
-----------------------------------------
Your 13-week project plan broken into tasks.

- Each week has a set of tasks.
- Tasks have statuses: TODO → IN_PROGRESS → DONE.
- Update task status as you progress.
- Completed weeks show as "done" on the ILM dashboard.

3.4 FINAL EXAM (/ilm/exam)
----------------------------
Unlocked when:
- Day 90+ reached.
- CA Score meets the threshold.

- Multiple-choice format.
- 1 attempt only.
- Pass mark: 70%.
- Completed attempt shows your score.

3.5 CERTIFICATE (/ilm/certificate)
------------------------------------
Generated automatically after passing the Final Exam.
- Unique Certificate ID shown.
- Download as PDF.
- Share directly to LinkedIn.
- Verifiable at /verify/:certId.

SECTION 7: KEY CONCEPTS & GLOSSARY
================================================================

ATS SCORE
  Applicant Tracking System score. Calculated based on skills match between candidate profile and job requirements. Range: 0–100.

OVERALL SCORE
  Composite score used in campus drives combining ATS score, CGPA, 10th%, 12th% against defined weights.

CA SCORE (Continuous Assessment)
  Running performance score during an internship. Calculated from daily log evaluations by mentor. Must meet threshold to unlock Final Exam.

ILM (Internship Lifecycle Management)
  The 90-day structured internship program. Includes daily logs, WBS, monthly reviews, final exam, and certificate.

WBS (Work Breakdown Structure)
  Week-by-week task breakdown of the internship project. 13 weeks total.

PRO STUDENT
  Paid subscriber. Gets priority shortlisting, profile highlights, analytics, and early access.

MENTOR
  A platform user assigned to an intern to review daily logs and conduct monthly assessments.

INTERN
  A student who has accepted an internship offer. Has access to full ILM portal.

CAMPUS DRIVE
  A structured college hiring event managed by HireStorm. The flow is:
  Admin creates drive → JD sent to college → College shares form link with students → Students apply via form → Auto-shortlisting → Manual selection → Offer sent.

DRIVE STATUS FLOW
  DRAFT → JD_SENT → APPLICATIONS_OPEN → SHORTLISTING → SHORTLISTED → FURTHER_ROUNDS → COMPLETED (or CANCELLED)

SECTION 8: TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================

"Login not working"
  → Double check email and password.
  → Try password reset (contact admin if no reset flow available).
  → Make sure you are using the correct portal (student vs college).

"Company account stuck on PENDING"
  → Admin needs to approve your company. Wait 24 hours or contact admin.

"ILM portal showing No Active Internship"
  → You must ACCEPT the offer from your dashboard first.
  → Check the Dashboard for the offer banner.

"Cannot apply to a listing"
  → You may have already applied.
  → Check My Applications page.

"Exam not unlocked"
  → Day 90 must be reached.
  → CA Score must meet the threshold set by admin.
  → Contact your mentor if you think there is an error.

"College portal login failed"
  → Verify your college code (case-insensitive).
  → Contact HireStorm admin to reset credentials.

"Application form link not working (campus drive)"
  → The drive status must be APPLICATIONS_OPEN.
  → Contact your college TPO for the correct link.
